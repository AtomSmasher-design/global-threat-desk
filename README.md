# Global Threat Desk

A free, single-file emergency and major-incident desk for the UK. One HTML file of about 1 MB. Open it in any browser. The plans and checklists work with or without a signal; the live sections update while you are online.

**[Open it in your browser now](https://atomsmasher-design.github.io/global-threat-desk/Global_Threat_Desk_UK_Emergency_PREP_v3.16.html)**: works on phones, nothing to install.

**[Download v3.16](https://github.com/AtomSmasher-design/global-threat-desk/raw/main/Global_Threat_Desk_UK_Emergency_PREP_v3.16.html)** (1.09 MB, SHA-256 below)

## What it covers

- **Major incidents:** cyber attacks, espionage against technology firms, big IT outages (Microsoft, AWS, Google, Cloudflare) and attacks on infrastructure, from official sources, the security press and major outlets. Dated dossiers with evidence grades sit alongside a live wire.
- **UK warnings in one place:** Met Office weather warnings for every hazard (wind, rain, thunder, snow and ice, fog, heat), UKHSA cold- and heat-health alerts for England, flood warnings for England, Wales and Scotland, GOV.UK emergency alerts, the GB electricity grid, and a 48-hour forecast for your area. It says plainly when nothing is in force.
- **Blackout plan:** what to do in the first hour, the first day and the first three days, with a printable plan.
- **Water:** how much to store for your household, plus frozen and burst pipes and the 24-hour emergency line for every UK water company.
- **Food:** a 7-day hot-food menu cooked on a camping stove, with a shopping list, gas cartridge count, calories and protein worked out for your household.
- **Heat, medical, hygiene, power, documents and contacts:** checklists that work out quantities for your household and how many days you choose.
- **UK emergency numbers:** 999 and 112, 111, power cut 105 (Northern Ireland 03457 643 643), gas 0800 111 999 (Northern Ireland 0800 002 001), and Floodline.

## How to use it

1. Download `Global_Threat_Desk_UK_Emergency_PREP_v3.16.html` with the link at the top of this page.
2. Open it in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge). No installation and no account are needed.
   On a phone, if it opens as a page of code, it has opened in a text viewer: use the **Open it in your browser now** link above instead.
3. Keep a copy on your phone, on a USB stick, and print the survival pack.

## Privacy

There are no adverts, no tracking and no account. Your plan, kit list and saved reports stay in your own browser; use the export button to back them up.

The live sections read public feeds from your browser: official sources (Met Office, Environment Agency, Natural Resources Wales, SEPA, UKHSA, GOV.UK, Elexon, CISA, NCSC), news publishers and their Bluesky accounts, and, for sites a browser cannot read directly, public relay services. Those services see an ordinary web request from your connection. A forecast place you type is cut to a town or the first half of a postcode and is kept only on your device. You can switch relays or any source off in Live wire settings, and the offline tools keep working without them.
